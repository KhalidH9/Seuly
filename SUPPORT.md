# Support

Seuly is maintained by its community on a best-effort basis.

## Choose the right channel

- Setup and usage questions: [issue forms](https://github.com/KhalidH9/Seuly/issues/new/choose)
- Reproducible bugs: [issue forms](https://github.com/KhalidH9/Seuly/issues/new/choose)
- Feature ideas: submit a focused feature request before implementation
- Vulnerabilities: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue
- Contribution help: read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)

Before asking, search existing issues, read the development and
database guides, and try the latest `main`. For bugs, include the Seuly version
or commit, Xcode and iOS versions, expected and actual behavior, reproduction
steps, and sanitized logs. Never post credentials or personal financial data.

## Support boundaries

Maintainers cannot access or administer your Supabase project, recover its
credentials, restore its backups, or guarantee response times. Questions about
Supabase billing, availability, or platform incidents belong with Supabase.

Seuly provides organizational information, not accounting, tax, investment,
legal, or other professional advice. Verify important financial decisions with
a qualified professional.
